## Context

On the EPM account, design and development teams were building solutions from static brand guidelines in PDF format: every time a component was needed, it had to be rebuilt from scratch, causing visual inconsistencies, duplicated effort across vendors, and contractual delays. I built and documented the account's first master library of living components.

## Leadership and governance

I catalogued every component, form, modal, and table across EPM's active applications to structure the parameterized digital library under WCAG accessibility standards, normalizing over 50% of the account's UI patterns. For the design and development team, that meant no longer rebuilding the same thing over and over, and gaining the confidence of working from a shared, reliable foundation. I represented Intergrupo in technical alignment sessions with EPM's other technology vendors to drive adoption of a single design system, and mentored junior designers on the team, strengthening their product design and business-thinking skills.
