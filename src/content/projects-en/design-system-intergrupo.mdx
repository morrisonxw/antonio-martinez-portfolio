## Context

On the EPM account, design and development teams were building solutions from static brand guidelines in PDF format: every time a component was needed, it had to be rebuilt from scratch, causing visual inconsistencies and duplicated effort across vendors. I built and documented the account's first master library of living components.

## Leadership and governance

I catalogued every component across EPM's active applications to build the parameterized digital library, normalizing over 50% of the account's UI patterns. I led alignment sessions with external technology vendors to drive adoption of the system beyond our own squad, and mentored junior designers into a culture of scalable systems instead of one-off, screen-by-screen solutions.
