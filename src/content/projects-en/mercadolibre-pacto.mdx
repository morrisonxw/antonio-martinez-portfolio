## Business context

Each transport mode (first-, mid-, and last-mile) runs on heterogeneous rate schemes — per kilometer, per package, mixed schemes, or local addenda — and recording those agreements between Mercado Libre and thousands of carriers was managed manually and informally, over email and disconnected spreadsheets. Each analyst spent up to 6 hours a day processing those schemes, switching between multiple non-integrated tools.

## Research and design decision

Discovery showed that the biggest source of error and time investment wasn't creating a single new rate — it was mass updates driven by fuel price swings or inflation, precisely because they were done by hand, row by row, in a shared spreadsheet.

<ProductDivider eyebrow="Insight" label="The biggest risk wasn't negotiating a new rate — it was updating thousands of existing rates without introducing an error." />

We digitized and centralized the full lifecycle of commercial agreements — creation, modification, indexing by inflation or fuel, renewal, and termination — with real-time business-rule validation. For mass updates, I designed a matrix-upload module that flags erroneous cells (currency formats, overlapping dates) directly in the interface, before they hit backend financial databases, instead of surfacing the error after it had already affected an invoice.

## Result

The team went from hours of manual processing per analyst to a real-time validated flow, and billing stopped carrying gaps caused by outdated rates. The solution also structured a commercial audit trail and freed up internal teams' capacity to focus on strategic cost analysis.
