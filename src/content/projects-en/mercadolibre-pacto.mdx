## Business context

Negotiating and recording rate agreements between Mercado Libre and thousands of carriers was managed in a fragmented way, over email and local spreadsheets. Each analyst spent several hours a day manually processing per-kilometer rate schemes, per-package schemes, mixed schemes, and local taxes.

## Research and design decision

Discovery showed that mass rate updates — driven by fuel price swings or inflation — caused the most errors, precisely because they were done by hand, row by row, in a shared spreadsheet.

<ProductDivider eyebrow="Insight" label="The biggest risk wasn't negotiating a new rate — it was updating thousands of existing rates without introducing an error." />

We digitized and centralized the full lifecycle of commercial agreements — creation, modification, indexing, and termination — with real-time business-rule validation. For mass updates, I designed a matrix-upload system that flags inconsistencies in the interface before they hit backend financial databases, instead of surfacing the error after it had already affected an invoice.
