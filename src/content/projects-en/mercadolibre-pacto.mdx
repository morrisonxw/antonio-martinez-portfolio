## Business context

Each transport mode (first-, mid-, and last-mile) runs on heterogeneous rate schemes: per kilometer, per package, mixed schemes, or local addenda, and recording those agreements between Mercado Libre and thousands of carriers was managed manually and informally, over email and disconnected spreadsheets. Each analyst spent up to 6 hours a day processing those schemes, switching between multiple non-integrated tools.

## My role

**UX/UI Designer / Mentor.** I took part in the initiative from a UX/UI design perspective at two points: initially with high involvement, defining the product's early hypotheses alongside product and engineering; later, once the product had matured, I adapted the solution to each country's and mile's specific cases. Across both moments, I mentored a Junior designer.

## Research and design decision

Discovery showed that the biggest source of error and time investment wasn't creating a single new rate: it was mass updates driven by fuel price swings or inflation, precisely because they were done by hand, row by row, in a shared spreadsheet.

<ProductDivider eyebrow="Insight" label="The biggest risk wasn't negotiating a new rate: it was updating thousands of existing rates without introducing an error." />

We digitized and centralized the full lifecycle of commercial agreements, equipping the system to handle cases like the operating-cost adjustments carriers face from inflation or fuel increases, plus real-time business-rule validation. For mass updates, we worked on a flow that shaped the matrix upload and flagged erroneous cells (currency formats, overlapping dates) directly in the interface, before they hit backend financial databases: that let us catch the error before it affected an invoice.

## Result

The team went from hours of manual processing per analyst to a real-time validated flow, and billing stopped carrying gaps caused by outdated rates. For analysts, that meant leaving behind repetitive row-by-row data entry and auditing their peers' uploads, freeing up time for other processes in the area that had been getting less attention. The solution also structured a commercial audit trail and freed up internal teams' capacity to focus on strategic cost analysis.
