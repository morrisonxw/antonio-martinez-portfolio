## Context

Commercial agreements with thousands of carriers were managed by hand, over email and spreadsheets, consuming hours of analyst time every day.

## My role

I was involved at two points, covering research and flow/interface design: early on, aligning first definitions with product and engineering from hypotheses, and guiding a junior designer in designing and shaping them; once the product matured, I came back to adapt the design to the specifics of each country and transport mode.

## How I worked

Discovery, done together with the business team, showed the biggest risk wasn't creating a new rate, but the mass updates done by hand, row by row, in a shared spreadsheet.

<ProductDivider eyebrow="Insight" label="The biggest risk wasn't negotiating a new rate: it was updating thousands of existing rates without introducing an error." />

We started from the hypothesis that errors were better prevented in the interface than after the data was loaded. I built the bulk-upload flow, prototyped the variants that flagged formatting errors before they could impact billing, and we tested them with analysts until it was ready to replace the shared spreadsheet.
